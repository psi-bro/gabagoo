Resources

Popular color grading apps and where to download them.

DaVinci Resolve

Industry-standard grading, free version available.

https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve

Adobe Premiere Pro

Editing with Lumetri color tools.

https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html

Adobe Lightroom

Photo grading and presets.

https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom.html

Final Cut Pro

Mac video editing with color wheels.

https://www.apple.com/final-cut-pro/

Capture One

Pro photo color editing.

https://www.captureone.com/

darktable

Free open-source photo workflow.

https://www.darktable.org/

Filmora

Beginner-friendly video editor with LUTs.

https://filmora.wondershare.com/

Lightworks

Editor with a free tier.

https://lwks.com/
